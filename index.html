Zhaofeng Sun 孙照峰

I am an undergraduate in Computer Science at Tsinghua University. Since June 2026, I have been conducting research with Prof. Hao Zhang at UC San Diego’s Hao AI Lab, working on efficient language model architectures and parallel decoding.

Previously, I worked with Prof. Beidi Chen at Carnegie Mellon University and Prof. Christopher De Sa at Cornell University. At Tsinghua, I worked with Prof. Jianfei Chen and Prof. Youyou Lu.

My research focuses on making language models more capable under practical compute, latency, and memory constraints. I work across model architecture, training algorithms, and GPU systems.

Email / CV / Google Scholar / GitHub

Research Interests

  • Efficient architectures: parallel and recurrent computation for language models.
  • Training and inference systems: sparse attention, long-context decoding, and efficient RL rollouts.
  • Model compression: quantization and structured sparsity through algorithm–hardware co-design.
