Zhaofeng Sun 孙照峰

I am an undergraduate in Computer Science at Tsinghua University. Since June 2026, I have been conducting research with Prof. Hao Zhang at UC San Diego’s Hao AI Lab, working on efficient language model architectures and parallel decoding.

Previously, I worked with Prof. Beidi Chen at Carnegie Mellon University and Prof. Christopher De Sa at Cornell University. At Tsinghua, I worked with Prof. Jianfei Chen.

My research focuses on making language models more capable under practical compute, latency, and memory constraints. I work across model architecture, training algorithms, and GPU systems.

Selected Research

ForesightLoop: Scaling Parallel Looped Transformers via Cross-Trajectory Latent Injection

Research project · UC San Diego · 2026

Reuses completed cross-trajectory representations to improve training stability and depth scaling while preserving parallel execution. With eight loops, improves average downstream accuracy by 0.84 percentage points over vanilla PLT with 0.3% additional decoding time.

Project & Demo/Code/Models

Sparrow: Sparse Rollout for Stable and Efficient Long-context RL of Large Language Models

Yang Zhou, Ranajoy Sadhukhan, Zhaofeng Sun, Zhuoming Chen, Souvik Kundu, Saket Dingliwal, Sai Muralidhar Jayanthi, Aram Galstyan, Haizhong Zheng, Beidi Chen

Preprint, 2026

A dynamic sparse-attention schedule controls actor-policy mismatch for stable long-context reinforcement learning, delivering up to 2.4× rollout speedup.

Model-Preserving Adaptive Rounding

Albert Tseng, Zhaofeng Sun, Christopher De Sa

ICML 2026

YAQA targets full-model output error during post-training quantization. Kronecker-factored Hessian sketches reduce KL divergence by approximately 30% over GPTQ/LDLQ without adding inference overhead.

Beyond 2:4: Exploring V:N:M Sparsity for Efficient Transformer Inference on GPUs

Kang Zhao, Tao Yuan, Han Bao, Zhenfeng Su, Chang Gao, Zhaofeng Sun, Zichen Liang, Liping Jing, Jianfei Chen
